credits

I made the original buttons as a set of around 30 pronoun and gender buttons for the profiles of my dragons on Flight Rising. I posted them in the forums for others to use, thinking maybe a few people would find it useful. I started getting requests for more flags, and then things that weren't gender/pronoun related. Other people started stepping in to host buttons, create new icons, design color palettes, and generate images with custom text. Things got out of hand and now here we are!

This project wouldn't have become what it is without the contributions of so many different people, and the support and encouragement of the community. Thank you all!
