-----
i've added a more explicit [url=https://idbuttons.neocities.org/license/]licensing agreement[/url] for the button assets - basically a share-alike system. anyone can use the buttons for free without credit. if you use my button bases or fonts to create custom stuff, you have to be okay with people using the stuff you make too, unless you ask me first. for most purposes i'll say yes, so don't worry! i just want to solidify my legal right to continue acting as an archivist for the database collection, and your legal right to keep using the buttons on websites/profiles/etc.
